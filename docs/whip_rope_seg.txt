whip_rope_seg - rope segment for whip_rope (v1, 2026-10-15)
===========================================================
One rigid rope segment, drawn once per pair of neighbouring Verlet nodes and placed every frame with SetEntityMatrix.

FILES (stream/)
  whip_rope_seg.ydr      the segment, casts shadows
  whip_rope_seg_ns.ydr   the same segment, no shadows (archetype flag 8192)
  whip_rope_seg.ytd      textures: whip_rope_seg_d (diffuse, DXT1 256x256), whip_rope_seg_n (normal, BC5 256x256)
  whip_rope_seg.ytyp     archetypes whip_rope_seg and whip_rope_seg_ns (lodDist 100, no physics)
  fxmanifest:  data_file 'DLC_ITYP_REQUEST' 'stream/whip_rope_seg.ytyp'

SHAPE (model space, scale 1)
  capsule along local +Y, origin in the middle
  body  y -0.125 .. +0.125 (0.25 m), radius 0.0175 (3.5 cm rope)
  caps  hemispheres of the rope's radius beyond each end (y reaches +/-0.1425)
  HD    12 sides, 3-ring caps, 168 triangles (to 15 m)
  LOD   6 sides, cone caps, 24 triangles (15 - 120 m)
  no collision

LOOK
  three-strand right-hand lay, warm tan #b48a55, strands in the normal map (normal.sps, bumpiness 1.2, matte)
  UV u runs around the rope (u = 0 at +Z, increasing towards +X), v along it; one texture tile = one lay with all 3
  strands; the body carries exactly 2 lays, so every segment starts and ends at the same strand phase

PLACING A SEGMENT between node a and node b
  centre   = (a + b) / 2
  forward  = (b - a) / |b - a|                     -> the matrix's Y axis, scaled by |b - a| / 0.25
  right/up = your rotation-minimising frame          -> X and Z axes, BOTH scaled by the same thickness factor
  roll 0 puts strand 1 at +Z (up)
  OVERLAP: use 0. The hemispherical caps close every joint by themselves: at Y scale |b - a| / 0.25 each cap is
  centred on its node, so neighbours' caps coincide and the strands meet in phase. Extra overlap moves the body past
  the node, which breaks the strand phase at the joint and makes a lump on the outside of bends (see the previews).
  Never scale X differently from Z: equal X/Z scale keeps the side normals perpendicular to Y, so lighting and the
  normal map hold under any Y stretch.

TANGENTS follow Rockstar's convention (checked against game drawables): tangent along +u, w = sign of
cross(tangent, normal) . dP/dv. Normal map is DirectX style (as Rockstar's).

Untested in game.

WINDING FIXED, 15 Oct 2026 (whip_rope; Josh: "it goes inside out and I see the bottom side")
  v1 had every HD triangle wound against its normals (144 of 144; the 24 others are zero-area cap tips) and
  half the LOD's (12 of 24). GTA culls by winding, so it drew the inside of the far wall: the underside, lit as
  the underside. Both .ydr were re-wound triangle by triangle so (p1-p0)x(p2-p0) agrees with the vertex normals,
  as every working model does; vertices, triangles, shader, textures, bounds and LODs are unchanged (checked
  field by field). Checker: the session scratchpad's ydrcheck (CodeWalker.Core). A rebuild from the v1 source
  needs the same fix there.
